01 — Problem

Launching tokens on XRPL is too hard

XRP Ledger has one of the fastest and cheapest native DEXes in crypto. With 3-5 second finality and fees under $0.001, it's perfectly built for token trading.

But launching a token today requires technical knowledge: setting up issuer wallets, trust lines, offer books, and managing multiple transactions manually. Most people give up before they start.

Meanwhile, Pump.fun on Solana processed $500M+ in volume — proving there's massive demand for simple token launching. XRPL has no equivalent. Until now.

One-click token launch on XRPL

PumpOnXRP abstracts all the complexity. Connect your wallet, fill in your token details, and launch — we handle the rest on-chain automatically.

03 — How It Works

Built on XRPL primitives

PumpOnXRP uses XRPL's native DEX and trust line system — no smart contracts, no bridges, no wrapped tokens.

Every token launched on PumpOnXRP is a real XRPL issued currency (IOU). Trading happens directly on the XRPL orderbook via OfferCreate transactions.

This means:

  • ✓ No smart contract risk — it's all native XRPL
  • ✓ Trades settle in 3-5 seconds
  • ✓ Fees under $0.001 per transaction
  • ✓ Fully non-custodial
  • ✓ Works with all XRPL wallets
